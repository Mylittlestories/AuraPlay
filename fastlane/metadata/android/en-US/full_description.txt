AuraPlay is an offline-first Android music player with a hi-fi sound engine, intelligent shuffle and mood-based playback.

Engineered by DRVsoft. Built on the open-source PixelPlayerOSS (GPL-3.0) — full credit and many thanks to @lostf1sh and its contributors.

Audiophile sound engine:
• AutoEQ headphone correction: curated profiles for 35+ popular headphones and earphones (Sennheiser HD series, Beyerdynamic DT series, Sony WH/WF, Hifiman, AKG, Moondrop, AirPods, Galaxy Buds and more)
• In-app preamp (−15…+12 dB) and brickwall true-peak limiter (−1 dBFS, 2.5 ms lookahead) — boosted EQ and loud masters can't clip
• Pure Direct mode: bypass every in-app processing stage for an untouched PCM path
• USB DAC / audio interface routing, float PCM output mode, hi-res sample-rate handling, FFmpeg decoders
• Equalizer with presets, crossfade and gapless playback, ReplayGain support

Playback intelligence:
• AuraShuffle: weighted whole-library shuffle with discovery boost and artist spacing — never the same twice
• Mood Radio: one tap builds a queue for your mood (chill, energy, happy, melancholy, discovery, favorites), fully offline

Visuals:
• Live spectrum visualizer in Now Playing, tinted by the album palette (no microphone permission — it taps the audio inside the app)
• Liquid-glass Now Playing with a breathing aura glow behind the album art
• Material 3 Expressive dynamic theming from album colors

Library & more:
• Albums, artists, genres, folders, favorites, playlists, instant search
• Lyrics (offline .lrc and optional LRCLIB lookup)
• Optional self-hosted sync: Navidrome/Subsonic, Jellyfin, ListenBrainz scrobbling
• Encrypted backup/restore, home-screen widgets, listening stats

No ads, no tracking, no account required. Online features are optional and off by default.
