AuraPlay is an offline-first Android music player with a hi-fi sound engine, intelligent shuffle and mood-based playback.

Engineered by DRVsoft. Built on the open-source PixelPlayerOSS (GPL-3.0) — full credit and many thanks to @lostf1sh and its contributors.

Sound engine:
• USB DAC / audio interface routing for the cleanest signal path
• Float PCM output mode, FFmpeg hi-res decoders
• Equalizer with presets, crossfade and gapless playback

Playback intelligence:
• AuraShuffle: weighted whole-library shuffle with discovery boost and artist spacing — never the same twice
• Mood Radio: one tap builds a queue for your mood (chill, energy, happy, melancholy, discovery, favorites), fully offline

Library & more:
• Albums, artists, genres, folders, favorites, playlists, instant search
• Lyrics (offline .lrc and optional LRCLIB lookup)
• Optional self-hosted sync: Navidrome/Subsonic, Jellyfin, ListenBrainz scrobbling
• Encrypted backup/restore, home-screen widgets, listening stats
• Material 3 dynamic theming, animated aura UI

No ads, no tracking, no account required. Online features are optional and off by default.
